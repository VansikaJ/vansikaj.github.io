02 — Proposed Solution

ANNAPURNA:
Grow up,
not out.

ANNAPURNA is a compact, modular vertical hydroponic farming system built for dense urban and semi-urban environments. Instead of spreading across acres of land, it stacks multiple plants into a small footprint — on your balcony, rooftop, or institution.

Built from accessible materials, automated with low-cost sensors, and designed to be repaired by anyone — it puts production-grade farming within reach of households, SHGs, and communities.

🌿

Modular PVC Frame

Small footprint, stackable growing levels. Assembled with standard plumbing parts — no special tools needed.

💧

Closed-Loop Nutrient System

150L reservoir recirculates nutrient solution through all levels — zero runoff, minimal waste, maximum efficiency.

⚡

Smart ESP32 Automation

pH, EC/TDS, and climate sensors feed a rules engine that automatically adjusts dosing, lighting, and ventilation in real time.

💡

5m of LED strips, 12–24V driver, timer or automation-controlled — enabling year-round growth independent of season.

03 — About Hydroponics

What is
hydroponics?

Plants don't actually need soil — they need nutrients, water, oxygen, and light. Hydroponics delivers all four directly, with zero waste and maximum precision.

🧪

No Soil Required

Plants grow in inert media (cocopeat or rockwool) with roots bathed in a precisely calibrated nutrient solution — getting exactly what they need, when they need it.

🔄

Recirculating Water

Nutrient solution flows from a central reservoir through all grow channels and returns back — using 80–90% less water than soil farming by eliminating evaporation and runoff.

📈

Accelerated Growth

With nutrients delivered directly to roots and optimal lighting conditions, hydroponic plants grow 30–50% faster and produce yields up to 11× higher than soil equivalents.

01

Germination

Seeds are placed in cocopeat-filled net cups. With daily moisture, roots emerge in 9–10 days.

02

Transfer to System

Healthy seedlings are inserted into the uPVC channels. The nutrient solution begins circulation immediately.

03

Active Growth

Sensors monitor pH and EC continuously. Automated peristaltic pumps dose nutrients to maintain ideal balance.

04

Harvest

Leafy greens are ready in 21–30 days. Fruiting crops in 37–60 days — far faster than soil equivalents.

08 — Honest Assessment

Known
Limitations

Every technology has constraints. Understanding them is part of designing solutions responsibly.

01

Initial Setup Investment

A fully equipped system costs ~₹21,738 upfront. While affordable compared to traditional farm infrastructure, it remains a barrier for very low-income households without microfinance support.

02

Power Dependency

The pump, LED lighting, and sensors require continuous electricity. Power outages can disrupt the system. Solar integration is planned but adds cost.

03

Crop Limitations

Root vegetables (potato, carrot, radish) and large grain crops are not suitable for this vertical NFT system. ANNAPURNA is best suited for leafy greens, herbs, and compact fruiting plants.

04

Technical Learning Curve

pH and EC monitoring requires basic training. Incorrect nutrient ratios can harm plants. The companion app and guided workflows aim to reduce this barrier significantly.

05

Nutrient Solution Disposal

Weekly nutrient solution replacement generates mild chemical wastewater. Proper disposal protocols are needed, especially at scale, to avoid soil or water contamination.

11 — Roadmap

Future
Improvements

ANNAPURNA is a living system. Each iteration gets smarter, more affordable, and more impactful.

Phase 1 — Near Term

Solar Integration

A 50W solar panel + battery backup eliminates grid dependency, making ANNAPURNA viable in power-unstable regions and dramatically reducing operating costs.

Phase 1 — Near Term

Rainwater Harvesting Coupling

Integration with a simple rooftop rainwater collection system for RO water supply — reducing recurring water input costs to near zero.

Phase 2 — Mid Term

AI-Driven Crop Intelligence

Computer vision-based plant health monitoring using camera modules — detecting deficiencies, diseases, and growth anomalies before they become problems.

Phase 2 — Mid Term

Aquaponics Integration

Adding a fish tank below the grow structure creates a symbiotic loop — fish waste feeds plants, plants filter water — enabling protein production alongside vegetables.

Phase 3 — Long Term

Community-Scale Clusters

Networked units sharing sensor data, nutrient supply, and logistics — creating micro food-production hubs at apartment complexes, schools, and community centers.

Phase 3 — Long Term

Bioregenerative Research

Collaborations with ISRO and space research institutions to adapt ANNAPURNA's closed-loop design for extra-terrestrial habitat life-support systems.
